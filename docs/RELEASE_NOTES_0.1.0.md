# Kairo 0.1.0

The first public release, in two editions built from the same code. Pick **one** jar:

- **`kairo-0.1.0.jar`**: Kairo Client. Frosted glass menus, HUD mods, PvP mode, cosmetics, emotes and visual
  tweaks.
- **`kairo-void-0.1.0.jar`**: Kairo Void, the visual edition. Everything Kairo has, plus energy blades, impact
  shockwaves, hit-stop, a cosmic night sky, ten atmospheres, crystal styles, presets and flat dark menus.

Both are **visual and client-side only**: no cheats, no automation, no packet changes, and nothing is sent anywhere.

## ⚠ Photosensitivity warning

Both editions (Void much more so) have flashing lights, bright bursts of colour, screen shakes and fast-moving
effects. A very small number of people can have a seizure when they see flashing lights or patterns, even with no
history of epilepsy. If you or anyone in your family has had seizures or epilepsy, talk to a doctor before playing.
Stop right away if you feel dizzy or sick, or notice blurred vision, eye or muscle twitches, or confusion. The first-run
setup lets you choose **Fewer flashes** and **Reduce motion**; both can be changed any time in Visuals.

## Install

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) for Minecraft Java **26.3**.
2. Put these in your `mods` folder:
   - [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) for 26.3
   - [Sodium](https://modrinth.com/mod/sodium) 0.9.x for 26.3 (required)
   - **one** of the two jars below (not both)
3. Start the game. A short setup runs the first time; in game, press **Right Shift**.

Windows is the only platform it has been tested on.

## Highlights

- Right Shift ring menu that breathes, glass (or flat Void) panels, five main menu styles, a first-run setup.
- 25+ mods with a drag-and-drop HUD editor, and a PvP mode with its own settings and layout.
- Attack, mining and inspect animations, colour filters, bloom, motion blur, hit and kill effects, sound packs,
  projectile trails, item physics, jump and explosion effects.
- Cosmetics only you see: capes, wings, hats, bandanas, trails, and 37 emotes with favourites.
- Share your whole look as one code (Visuals → Share look).

See the README for the full list, and CHANGELOG.md for what's in this version.
