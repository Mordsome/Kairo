# Kairo 0.2.0

Your wallpaper's colours, a better CPS, and updates from inside the game (only if you want them). Both editions get
everything. Pick **one** jar:

- **`kairo-0.2.0.jar`**: Kairo Client. Frosted glass menus, HUD mods, PvP mode, cosmetics, emotes and visual
  tweaks.
- **`kairo-void-0.2.0.jar`**: Kairo Void, the visual edition. Everything Kairo has, plus energy blades, impact
  shockwaves, a cosmic night sky, nine atmospheres, crystal styles, presets and flat dark menus.

Both are **visual and client-side only**: no cheats, no automation, no packet changes.

## ⚠ Photosensitivity warning

Both editions (Void much more so) have flashing lights, bright bursts of colour, screen shakes and fast-moving
effects. A very small number of people can have a seizure when they see flashing lights or patterns, even with no
history of epilepsy. If you or anyone in your family has had seizures or epilepsy, talk to a doctor before playing.
Stop right away if you feel dizzy or sick, or notice blurred vision, eye or muscle twitches, or confusion. The first-run
setup lets you choose **Fewer flashes** and **Reduce motion**; both can be changed any time in Visuals.

## New in 0.2.0

- **Wallpaper colours.** Like Material You: set any image, GIF or panorama as your main menu background and Kairo
  reads its colours. Click a swatch in Visuals → Theme (Kairo Void: Interface → Wallpaper colours) and the client
  takes it on: buttons, toggles and the ring in the main colour, panels in a dark tone of it, text in a very light
  tint. Switch on **Use wallpaper colours** to follow your background whenever it changes.
- **Make it yours.** Tint backgrounds and Tint text each have a switch and a strength slider, and every mod has
  **Follow theme colours** and **Tinted background** in its own settings. Text always stays readable (WCAG 4.5:1).
  Look codes now carry your colours and tints, never your picture.
- **CPS label.** Change the word after your CPS, or leave it empty for just `8 | 6`.
- **CPS in Keystrokes.** The mouse buttons can show your live CPS for each button, or a small label above the number.
  The separate CPS box steps aside while they do (optional), in the layout editor and in your PvP layout too.
- **Panel edge light switch.** The light that runs along the top of big panels can now be turned off on its own
  (Visuals → Menus).
- **Updates from inside the game, opt-in.** See below.

## Updates and privacy

Kairo still has no accounts, no telemetry and no analytics, and it stays offline by default. New in this version is
one switch, **Check for updates on GitHub**, which is **off** unless you turn it on (in the first-run setup or in
Settings → About). When it's on:

- Once per launch, Kairo asks GitHub for the latest release of this project. That request sends nothing about you or
  your game.
- If there's a newer version, the main menu tells you, and Settings → About shows it with its notes.
- Nothing is downloaded or installed until you click **Update**. Then Kairo downloads only your edition's jar from
  the release, checks it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and swaps it in after you close the game.

Profiles from 0.1.0 load with exactly the look they had: the new tints start off there (one switch in Visuals →
Theme turns them on).

## Install

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) for Minecraft Java **26.3**.
2. Put these in your `mods` folder:
   - [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) for 26.3
   - [Sodium](https://modrinth.com/mod/sodium) 0.9.x for 26.3 (required)
   - **one** of the two jars below (not both)
3. Start the game. A short setup runs the first time; in game, press **Right Shift**.

Updating from 0.1.0 by hand: remove the old `kairo-0.1.0.jar` (or `kairo-void-0.1.0.jar`) from `mods` and put the new
one in. Your settings stay.

Windows is the only platform it has been tested on.
