# Kairo 0.4.0

A lighter plain Kairo, new mods and fixes. Pick **one** jar:

- **`kairo-0.4.0.jar`**: Kairo Client. Frosted glass menus, HUD mods, PvP mode, cosmetics, emotes and visual tweaks.
- **`kairo-void-0.4.0.jar`**: Kairo Void, the visual edition, with every effect built in.

Both are **visual and client-side only**: no cheats, no automation, no packet changes.

## Animations and Combat Effects are addons now (plain Kairo)

Most people don't use every effect, so plain Kairo got lighter. Two addons bring them back:

- **Animations**: attack, mining and inspect animations, the swing trail and held item looks.
- **Combat Effects**: hit particles, kill effects, damage numbers, the edge flash and sound packs.

Install them in the [Kairo Launcher](https://github.com/Mordsome/KairoLauncher) (Addons), or put their jars from
[Mordsome/KairoAddons](https://github.com/Mordsome/KairoAddons/releases) in `mods`. **Your old choices are kept** and
come back as they were. Kairo Void has all of this built in, no addon needed.

## New

- **Screenshots page** with Favourites: every screenshot in a grid, big view, copy, open, delete.
- **Reconnect**: a button on the "Disconnected" screen to join the same server again (optional countdown, off by default).
- **Drop Warning**: asks before you drop netherite, diamonds, golden apples, totems and other valuables.
- **Ore Outline**: a thin outline round ores you can see, in each ore's colour. Nothing through walls.
- **Sizes** for the menus, the main menu and the HUD (now 130% by default, scaling the whole layout evenly, with − / +
  in the layout editor); the menus follow Minecraft's GUI Scale.
- **Restart the game** from Settings → About (with the Kairo Launcher).
- **Shortcuts** to the menus in the pause menu and the inventory (each one switchable), and a key for each page.
- **Themes from addons**, picked from pictures in Visuals → Theme: a theme now reaches everything (main menu, pause
  menu, ring, every menu and control, and the HUD). The new **Themes** addon has Glass, Minimal, Retro and Neon, in
  both editions.
- **The Kairo 98 addon**: Kairo, and Minecraft's own menus, inventories, hotbar and HUD icons, as a late-90s desktop
  with a taskbar and Start menu. Several colour schemes.
- **The Command Bar addon**: Right Shift can open a search bar instead of the ring. Your ring sections are big
  numbered tiles under it; type to find any page, mod or setting (Visuals → Ring → Right Shift opens).
- Smoother Mods search.

## Also new: finished after 0.3.0 shipped

These were part of 0.3.0's work but were finished after the 0.3.0 jars went out, so they arrive now.

- **Now Playing** (Windows). A HUD mod showing the song your PC is playing, from Spotify, YouTube or any player Windows
  knows about: cover, title, artist, app, progress and time, each with its own switch.
- **Music Visualiser** (Windows). Bars, a wave, dots or a ring on your HUD moving to the sound your PC plays.
  Both are off by default; while one is on, a small PowerShell helper that comes inside the jar reads Windows' media
  controls and sound level. It never goes online and stops with the game.
- **Time Changer and Weather Changer.** Pick the time of day and the weather you see. Only your own picture changes.
- **Addons.** Kairo can take addons: small extras from [Mordsome/KairoAddons](https://github.com/Mordsome/KairoAddons),
  such as a different look for the menus (Plastic Menus).
- **The held item outline** (both editions) is now a real outline that follows the item's shape.
- **More in Kairo Void:** starfall and sky crystals, a pulse grid, rivers of light, shapes and rifts in the sky,
  ripples under your steps, rush streaks, blocks that shatter into light and pulse when placed, a weapon aura that
  charges with your attack, a crown of light for kill streaks and an echo of what you hit.
- On a **new** Kairo Void install every visual starts off, so you switch on what you want. Existing settings stay.

## Changed

- The **loading screen** takes your accent colour: a deep shade of it behind, the Kairo wordmark in it.
- **Kairo Void** no longer plays its "Kairo Void" start-up intro: the loading screen leads straight to the menu.

## Fixed

- Stray dots in the inventory, grey boxes behind the furnace's flame and arrow, and the green recipe book button.
- Animated capes flickering with Chroma or Gradient colours.

## Install

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) for Minecraft Java **26.3**.
2. Put these in your `mods` folder:
   - [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) for 26.3
   - [Sodium](https://modrinth.com/mod/sodium) 0.9.x for 26.3 (required)
   - **one** of the two jars below (not both)
3. Start the game. In game, press **Right Shift**.

Updating: remove the old Kairo jar from `mods` and put the new one in, or use **Update** in Settings → About if you
turned on update checks. Your settings stay.
