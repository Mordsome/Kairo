# Kairo 0.3.0

Kairo Void gets a whole new layer of visuals that don't look like Minecraft at all, and both editions get music
emotes. Pick **one** jar:

- **`kairo-0.3.0.jar`**: Kairo Client. Frosted glass menus, HUD mods, PvP mode, cosmetics, emotes and visual
  tweaks.
- **`kairo-void-0.3.0.jar`**: Kairo Void, the visual edition. Everything Kairo has, plus energy blades, impact
  shockwaves, a cosmic night sky, nine atmospheres, crystal styles, presets, flat dark menus and everything below.

Both are **visual and client-side only**: no cheats, no automation, no packet changes.

## ⚠ Photosensitivity warning

Both editions (Void much more so) have flashing lights, bright bursts of colour, screen shakes and fast-moving
effects. A very small number of people can have a seizure when they see flashing lights or patterns, even with no
history of epilepsy. If you or anyone in your family has had seizures or epilepsy, talk to a doctor before playing.
Stop right away if you feel dizzy or sick, or notice blurred vision, eye or muscle twitches, or confusion. The first-run
setup lets you choose **Fewer flashes** and **Reduce motion**; both can be changed any time in Visuals. Every new effect
in this version follows them.

## New in Kairo Void

Everything here is drawn with Kairo's own geometry and shaders, not vanilla particles, and only on your screen. Each one
has its own card in Visuals (most in the new **You** group).

- **Start-up intro.** When the game opens, "Kairo" rises into place and "Void" pops in next to it. Once per launch;
  click to skip.
- **Your own sky.** Any picture as the sky (a wide 2:1 panorama wraps all the way round), slowly turning, by day, by
  night or both, with the sun, moon and stars on top if you like.
- **Living aurora.** Ribbons of light winding across the whole night sky, with light running along them. Green and
  violet, your colour, rainbow or sunset.
- **Aura and line glyphs.** Orbiting cubes, light ribbons, prism shards or halo rings around you, and animated angular
  lines turning at your feet, waist and over your head.
- **Alive through your own eyes.** In first person your aura and glyphs spread out a few blocks round you, never in
  your face.
- **Light ribbons and motes.** Ribbons of light hang and twist in the air all around the world, like an aurora you walk
  through, with small lights drifting close by. Brightest at night.
- **Light particles.** Hits, kills, explosions, totems, atmospheres and trails become sparks, shards and rings of light
  instead of Minecraft's pixel particles.
- **Light trails.** A fading ribbon, comet, twin streaks or prism trail behind you.
- **Your look.** An outline, a glow or a hologram on your own player (never on anyone else).
- **Hit impacts.** Shards, a sigil, a cross slash, a star or rings of light where your hit lands.
- **Animated damage markers.** Numbers punch in, slash, stack into combos and break apart; crits get spikes and a tag.
- **Cinematic kill.** After a kill a drawing (white with black outlines by default) spreads out from it like a wave of
  ink, then draws back in. Slow and smooth or sharp and quick; with Fewer flashes it's always smooth and calm grey.
- **Favourites.** Visuals opens on your favourite effects, the most important ones already starred. Right-click any
  card to add or remove it.
- **Block overlay.** Brackets, a scan, a hologram grid or a pulse on the block you look at, gliding smoothly from block
  to block.
- **Nametags.** Glass, Neon, Hologram or Shimmer names. They show exactly when Minecraft would show them.

## New in both editions

- **Music emotes.** Two emotes that play your own song (OGG or WAV) while you dance to it. Set the song's BPM and a beat
  offset and the dance lands on the beat; pick the dance, including two new beat dances. Only you hear it. Add songs in
  Right Shift → Cosmetics → Emotes.

Settings from 0.2.0 carry over. In Kairo Void a few of the new effects start on (the intro, the ribbon aurora, light
ribbons, motes, light particles, line glyphs, the bracket block overlay, glass nametags, shard hit impacts and the animated
damage markers); the rest start off. Switch any of them in Visuals.

## Install

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) for Minecraft Java **26.3**.
2. Put these in your `mods` folder:
   - [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) for 26.3
   - [Sodium](https://modrinth.com/mod/sodium) 0.9.x for 26.3 (required)
   - **one** of the two jars below (not both)
3. Start the game. A short setup runs the first time; in game, press **Right Shift**.

Updating: remove the old Kairo jar from `mods` and put the new one in, or use **Update** in Settings → About if you
turned on update checks. Your settings stay.

Windows is the only platform it has been tested on. Kairo is built and tested on Minecraft's OpenGL renderer; if you
switched Minecraft to Vulkan and something looks wrong, switch Video Settings back to OpenGL.
